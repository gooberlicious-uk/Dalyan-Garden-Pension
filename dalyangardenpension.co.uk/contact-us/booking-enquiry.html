About bookings
In general we do not ask for a deposit, as we use your flight details as confirmation. We confirm your stay once you have booked your flights and you have given us full details. We can pencil and hold rooms whilst you book your flights with your operator but we cannot guarantee to hold rooms without flight confirmations for more than ten days. Please pay for accommodation and transfers on arrival. If you prefer to pay in an alternative currency please let us know at the time of booking and we can confirm the price. We reserve the right to change our payment terms and ask for a specific currency if required.

Airport transfers are available on request. Prices are dependent on the size of the party, but for two to three people the cost is approximately £65 return.

Unfortunately we cannot offer self catering facilities; meals and drinks to be consumed on the premises should be purchased from us.

All prices include Turkish breakfast (bread, cheese, jam, eggs, seasonal fruit, tea, coffee or fruit juice). Additional breakfast items may be available on request at an extra charge.
Lunch snacks are available from the bar until 3:00pm. We do not provide evening meals, though we can organise food for you if you prefer not to go out in the evening.

Drinks and food are usually charged at the end of your stay. If you prefer to pay daily or as you go please let us know.
