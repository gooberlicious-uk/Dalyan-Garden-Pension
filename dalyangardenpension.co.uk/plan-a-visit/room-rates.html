Room rates

Please book directly to obtain the best room rates and for special offers.

Book now
Please contact us regarding rates and availability. Access to room is from 2pm on day of arrival. Vacate rooms by 11am on day of departure. If you are taking night flights you may need to book for the day of travel and/or day of departure if you would like access to a room outside of these hours. We are happy to discuss this.

In general we do not ask for a deposit, as we use your flight details as confirmation. We confirm your stay once you have booked your flights and you have given us full details.

We can pencil and hold rooms whilst you book your flights with your operator but we cannot guarantee to hold rooms without flight confirmations for more than ten days.

Airport transfers are available on request. Prices are dependent on the size of the party, but for up to five people the cost is approximately £75 return.

Unfortunately we cannot offer self catering facilities; drinks to be consumed on the premises should be purchased from us.

All prices include Turkish breakfast (bread, cheese, jam, eggs, seasonal fruit, tea, coffee or fruit juice). Additional breakfast items may be available on request at an extra charge.
Lunch snacks are available from the bar until 2.30 pm. We offer an evening BBQ approximately once a week otherwise we do not provide evening meals, though we can organise food for you if you prefer not to go out in the evening.

Drinks and food are usually charged at the end of your stay. If you prefer to pay daily or as you go please let us know.
