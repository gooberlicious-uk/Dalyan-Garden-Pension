Rooms

The pension has ten simple but comfortable bedrooms set over two floors.

Downstairs there are three rooms – two standard doubles and a twin, each with a private terrace overlooking the garden, air-conditioning, en-suite bathroom, and walk-in wardrobe.

Upstairs there are seven rooms – Three spacious superior double rooms, all with Juliet balconies, and sofa beds which make them suitable for triple occupancy, and en-suite bathrooms.One standard double room and three twin rooms open onto a communal balcony set with tables and chairs offering views over the garden. These rooms have en-suite bathrooms and walk in wardrobes.

All rooms have air-conditioning. Hairdryers are provided and a tray with a kettle, cups and saucers can be provided on request.Rooms are cleaned daily, sheets and towels are changed every three or four days or more frequently on request.
