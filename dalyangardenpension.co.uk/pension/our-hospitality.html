Our Hospitality

Breakfast is served in the garden (table service rather than a buffet). Guests just have to choose somewhere to sit and a traditional Turkish breakfast, included in the room price, will be served to them anytime between 8:30am and 10:30am. Fresh products from our garden or from the local market are included - tomatoes, cucumber, olives, fresh fruit, cheese and jams.

Soft drinks, beers, wines, lunch snacks and ice creams are available from the bar throughout the day, (lunch served until 3:00pm). The menu includes simple Turkish recipes such as menamen, sandwiches, omelettes and salads. We offer a good selection of beers, cider, wines, spirits and liqueurs to enjoy during the day and evening.

We host an evening three course BBQ, usually one night a week, serving a selection of freshly made Turkish dishes, a choice of meat or fish mains and a dessert. Although we do not otherwise prepare evening meals we can organise food for you if you prefer not to go into town.

The bar is open throughout the day and through to one o’clock in the morning. Guests can enjoy good company over a beer or gin and tonic at sundown or with a night cap after a dinner out in town. There is also a comfortable reception lounge and free library/book exchange for guests use. We can arrange car hire, excursions, boat trips, visits to Turkish baths etc. at no additional cost than booking yourself directly.

Bicycles are available for a small one off charge to cover maintenance. Please let us know when you check in if you would like to use the bikes. The charge is £5 or 20TL per person for the duration of your stay. The use of bikes is on a first come first served basis. We are also happy to advise on local transport, tours and activities or help with car hire.

Wi-Fi is available throughout the pension rooms and gardens.
