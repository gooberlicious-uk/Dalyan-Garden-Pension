Pool and Garden

The raised seventeen metre pool is just a few steps across the grass from your room and is a very generous size for a pension that can accommodate a maximum of twenty four guests. There’s always plenty of room to cool off and ample sun beds around the poolside. The water is shallow towards the edges of the pool (max 1m) but deeper in the centre (max 1.4m) so is equally suitable for those who wish to swim lengths and for those less confident in the water. It is ideal for quiet relaxation but not designed for jumping or diving.

The Mediterranean garden is filled with sunflowers, lilies, palms, banana and citrus trees, flowering shrubs and conifers. While at its brightest in May and June the garden remains lush and green throughout the season. There are shady seating areas around the garden including a kosh, a cushion filled soft area, where guests can take a nap, read, or play a game of cards. We have an extensive lending and exchange library in reception, and our Wi-Fi covers the garden and pool area.
