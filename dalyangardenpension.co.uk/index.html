Welcome

Dalyan Garden Pension is a small family run hotel set in the rural Arikbasi area of Dalyan. Since opening in 2005 it has featured in many guide books such as Cadogan’s Guide to Turkey, Guide Evasion, Guide Hachette and the Sunflower Guide - Kas to Dalyan. It has also featured in the Sunday Times Travel magazine, the Daily Telegraph Travel Section and an airline in-flight magazine.

The pension has become a firm favourite with many guests who we welcome back year after year. The ten simple rooms set facing a seventeen metre pool, shady garden and convivial bar offer comfortable informal accommodation. From the upstairs balcony there are lovely views of the surrounding mountains and farmland. It is the perfect location whether you just to want to unwind - reading in the shade of the garden, sunbathing or cooling off in the pool, or whether you prefer to spend time at the beach or explore the surrounding countryside.
